Where the lots come from

One source today. Titles, bids and close times only — the photos stay on the auction site.

Every lot on Deals is a live listing on GovDeals, the marketplace where cities, counties, school districts, police departments and state agencies sell what they no longer need: fleet vehicles, office computers, shop tools, confiscated goods, whole storerooms at a time.

We store the text of each listing — title, location, current bid, bid count, close time — and the outcome once it closes. We do not republish the seller's photos or descriptions in the feed; the lot page links straight to the source so you bid there, under GovDeals' own terms and buyer's premium.

You will not find chairs, benches or banquet seating here. Seating is our own trade; we don't publish the lots we bid on.
