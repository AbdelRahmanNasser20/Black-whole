How these numbers are made

  • Sold price = the winning bid on a lot the auction site marked as sold. Buyer premium and tax are not included.
  • Not a sale: a lot that ended under its reserve, was cancelled, or got no bid. These lots are counted, not priced.
  • Sold share = sales ÷ (sales + lots that ended under the reserve).
  • US dollars only. Median and middle half (25th–75th percentile) are used because some lots sell very high.
  • Records start on {{ data.summary.since or '—' }}. Sites that do not publish a close record are not in this table.
