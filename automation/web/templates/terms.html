DEPOSITS & REFUNDS

{{ policy }}

  1. 01 · BEFORE THE TRUCK IS BOOKED

    Fully refundable. Change your mind before we schedule a carrier and you get every cent back, to the original payment method. No restocking fee, no questions.

  2. 02 · AFTER THE TRUCK IS BOOKED

    If you cancel once freight is booked, the deposit covers the freight cost. That booking is a real commitment we make to a carrier on your behalf, and it is not refundable to us either. Anything left over after the carrier is paid comes back to you.

  3. 03 · THE DEPOSIT IS NOT A FEE

    It applies to your balance. A $450 deposit on a $3,000 order means $2,550 due at delivery — the deposit is money off what you owe, never money on top of it.

  4. 04 · THE BALANCE

    Payable at delivery or pickup, by Zelle or cash. You see the chairs before the rest of the money moves.

  5. 05 · RAILS

    Deposits can be paid by card or bank transfer. Pay-in-full is bank transfer only — card fees on a full order are real money, and we would rather put that into the price. Bank transfers take 1–4 business days to clear; the lot is held for you from the moment payment is initiated.

  6. 06 · REFUNDS

    Issued to the original payment method. Card refunds land in a few business days; bank refunds can take up to a week, depending on your bank, not us.

  7. 07 · IF WE CAN'T DELIVER

    If a lot turns out to be short, damaged, or gone before we hand it over, you get a full refund regardless of anything above. We don't keep money for chairs you never got.

FREIGHT ESTIMATES

  • The number a lot page gives you is an estimate, not a booked rate. It is finalized at ship time, when we tender the real pallet count and dimensions to a carrier.

  • Estimates assume residential delivery with a liftgate — the expensive version. A dock or a forklift on your end usually makes it cheaper, not dearer.

  • The chair price is separate and firm. Freight moves with fuel and lane; the price per chair does not.

  • Local pickup is always free. Bring a truck to the lot's city and freight is a line item that never happens.

  • Lanes we can't price honestly — international, offshore, or an address we can't resolve — get a hand quote instead of a guess. We would rather make you wait a day than give you a number that changes.
