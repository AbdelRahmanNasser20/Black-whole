      half hour). Every change of price, bid count or leader is kept, so a closed
      lot shows its final price and the masked handle that won it. Starred
      favorites join automatically under favorites.

      and the LLM never runs. Cards whose title doesn't mention the keyword
      get a ⚠ off-keyword flag so you can
      judge how noisy the term is.
