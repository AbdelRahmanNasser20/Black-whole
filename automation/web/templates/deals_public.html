What this is Government surplus, priced by the unit.

Surplus Radar sweeps every live GovDeals auction every six hours, follows each lot to its close, and records how it ended — the no-bids, the $1 wins, the final prices GovDeals itself never publishes. Lots from GSA Auctions, Purple Wave, AllSurplus (US only) and other public surplus sites ride along from the same recorder.

It reads the quantity out of the listing text so a "Lot of (286) HP EliteOne" is priced per machine, not per lot, and adds buyer premium so the number you see is what you'd actually pay.

  1. Sweep the whole site through the public search API, every 6 h
  2. Watch each lot's clock to its close; anti-snipe extensions included
  3. Record outcome + final bid per auction — relists never overwrite history
  4. Price quantity from the title → $/unit, plus 12.5 % buyer premium → landed
