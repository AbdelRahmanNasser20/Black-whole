WHAT THIS IS — tap to collapse

Government surplus, priced by the unit.

Surplus Radar sweeps every live GovDeals auction every six hours, follows each lot to its close, and records how it ended — the no-bids, the $1 wins, the final prices GovDeals itself never publishes.

It reads the quantity out of the listing text so a "Lot of (286) HP EliteOne" is priced per machine, not per lot, and adds buyer premium so the number you see is what you'd actually pay.
