## What this pass proves about each vertical

- **tools_shop** — every single DeWalt lot clears cost even at 50% as-is
  haircuts. Highest confidence vertical; bid these routinely.
- **medical/lab** — the asymmetry king (Valleylab, exam table) but requires
  the legality check (Class I / openly-traded Class II like ESUs and infant
  warmers are eBay-legal with the FDA-purchaser disclaimer) and a power-on
  test policy.
- **kitchen** — refrigeration is the landmine exactly as researched: milk
  coolers (simple, 115V) = buy; merchandisers/reach-ins = only if heard
  running; sellers who open at dealer-retail (Montague) = ignore.
- **comms_radios** — knowledge-gated as predicted. Cheap "working + ships"
  lots are free money; big contested-brand lots need band-split homework.
- **music** — exact-model comps exist even for a 1900s Belgian tuba; school
  surplus is mispriced because nobody comps it.
- **fitness** — brand hierarchy is everything (Hammer Strength ≫ TuffStuff at
  the same opener).

## Standing comp rules validated this pass

1. Seller's opening price ≠ market: Montague opened ABOVE used-dealer price,
   MTS2000s opened at 1% of value. The tracker's job is telling these apart.
2. The 40–60% as-is discount held across every category with live comps.
3. Completeness (batteries, remote, cart, footswitch, rotor) moved value
   30–100% in 6 of 23 lots.
4. Power requirements and "does it run" gate all refrigeration/engine lots.
